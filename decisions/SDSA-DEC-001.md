## Decision

Run two connected but separately governed tracks:

1. Make the upcoming class the immediate enrollment campaign and primary website/Facebook-ad conversion offer.
2. Explore the affordable subsidized boys' and girls' club as a separate strategic track. Do not present teams or the club as available until its scope, economics, operations, and launch decision are approved.

## Consequences

- The class relaunch does not wait for a complete club plan.
- The public experience gets one clear near-term conversion action.
- Club interest may be collected only with accurate exploratory language.
- No team availability, age-group coverage, price, subsidy, sponsorship, or launch timing may be invented.
- The pre-SpecLoops website build remains prototype evidence and may be reused only through a future approved Build Assignment.

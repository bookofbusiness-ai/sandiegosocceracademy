# Decision Record: 2026 Class Offer and Registration Boundary

**Decision ID:** `SDSA-DEC-002`  
**Status:** `CANONICAL`  
**Decided:** 2026-09-17  
**Authority:** direct human confirmation in the SpecLoops Product Room  
**Campaign:** `SDSA-CAMPAIGN-002`

## Decision

- The supplied class listings are for 2026.
- The internal campaign enrollment floor is 6.
- Final registration remains on the City of Chula Vista's public website.
- The academy website may act as an explanatory conversion funnel and hand families off to the City via a prominent `Sign up now` action.

## Evidence boundary

The supplied City listings display `Min Participants 12` and `Max Participants 36`. The human-defined floor of 6 is recorded as an internal campaign threshold; it does not assert that the City's displayed program minimum has changed.

## Proposed experiment

Compare two otherwise equivalent ad paths:

1. Facebook ad -> academy class landing experience -> City registration.
2. Facebook ad -> City registration directly.

This experiment remains `PROPOSED` until its budget, audience, duration, tracking, destination URLs, and success measure are approved in Q3.

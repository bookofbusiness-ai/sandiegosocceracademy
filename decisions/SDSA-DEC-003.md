# Decision Record: Two-Destination Acquisition Test

**Decision ID:** `SDSA-DEC-003`  
**Status:** `CANONICAL_STRUCTURE`  
**Decided:** 2026-09-17  
**Authority:** direct human approval in the SpecLoops Product Room  
**Campaign:** `SDSA-CAMPAIGN-002`

## Decision

Approve a controlled test of two registration paths:

1. Facebook ad -> academy class landing experience -> City registration.
2. Facebook ad -> City registration directly.

Audience, creative, schedule, and budget allocation must remain equal so destination is the controlled variable.

## Measurement boundary

- Variant A may measure the outbound click from the academy site to the City.
- Final registration is owned by the City system, so click-through rate alone is not the success measure.
- Compare attributable City enrollment and cost per enrollment where reliable source evidence is available.
- If only the general City catalog is available, record its added search friction rather than treating it as equivalent to a direct class page.

## Authority boundary

This decision approves the test structure only. It does not authorize:

- advertising spend;
- ad publication;
- website implementation;
- tracking deployment;
- audience targeting;
- campaign launch.

Those actions require Q3 to be completed and an approved Build Assignment or launch authorization.

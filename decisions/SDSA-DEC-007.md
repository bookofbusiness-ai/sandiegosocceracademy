# Decision Record: Registration Outcome Without a Fixed Threshold

**Decision ID:** `SDSA-DEC-007`  
**Status:** `CANONICAL`  
**Decided:** 2026-09-17  
**Authority:** direct human approval in the SpecLoops Product Room  
**Campaign:** `SDSA-CAMPAIGN-002`

## Decision

Retire the previously recorded internal enrollment threshold of 6. Promote the class and evaluate the approved acquisition test by:

- class registrations produced;
- cost per attributable registration where reliable evidence exists;
- supporting funnel diagnostics such as academy-to-City outbound clicks.

No fixed minimum number of registrations controls campaign success.

## Preserved external fact

The City listing displays `Min Participants 12`. That remains external source evidence and is not changed by this decision.

## Boundary

The approved $200 spending cap remains in force. “Ditch the limit” is interpreted as removing the enrollment threshold, not removing the advertising budget cap.

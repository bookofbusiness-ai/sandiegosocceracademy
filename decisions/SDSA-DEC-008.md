# Decision Record: Adult Advertising Audience

**Decision ID:** `SDSA-DEC-008`  
**Status:** `CANONICAL`  
**Decided:** 2026-09-17  
**Authority:** direct human approval in the SpecLoops Product Room  
**Campaign:** `SDSA-CAMPAIGN-002`

## Decision

Target the approved acquisition test to:

- adults ages 25-65+;
- all genders;
- people within 30 miles of Salt Creek Park;
- messaging relevant to parents, guardians, and grandparents who may choose or pay for a child's class.

Do not directly target minors.

## Boundary

This decision defines the eligible adult audience. It does not authorize advertising publication, spend, tracking deployment, or campaign launch.

# Decision Record: Privacy-Light Campaign Measurement

**Decision ID:** `SDSA-DEC-010`  
**Status:** `CANONICAL`  
**Decided:** 2026-09-17  
**Authority:** direct human approval in the SpecLoops Product Room  
**Campaign:** `SDSA-CAMPAIGN-002`

## Decision

Measure the acquisition test without Meta Pixel, advertising cookies, visitor profiles, or personal-data transmission from the academy site.

Use:

- aggregate Meta campaign statistics;
- anonymous counts of academy-site `Sign up now` clicks;
- City registration totals as final outcome evidence.

## Boundary

- Do not upload contact lists or create custom audiences from academy family data.
- Do not claim individual registration attribution when the City system does not provide it.
- Do not collect additional child or family information for campaign measurement.
- Measurement implementation remains unauthorized until an approved Build Assignment is issued.

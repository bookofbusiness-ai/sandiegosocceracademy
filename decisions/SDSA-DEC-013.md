# Decision Record: Founder Identity and Bookable Coach Platform

**Decision ID:** `SDSA-DEC-013`
**Recorded:** 2026-09-17
**Status:** `CANONICAL_PRODUCT_CAPABILITY`

## Decision

Jason Taylor, Jeff Taylor, Ashley Hernandez-Cavanagh, and Greg Coco are current founders/members of San Diego Soccer Academy. Founder/member identity is separate from private-coaching availability. None of the four should be presented as currently bookable solely because they are founders or appear on a legacy website.

The private-training experience must support a managed roster of available coaches. Each published coach receives an individual, San Diego Soccer Academy-branded booking page where a parent or guardian can:

- read a verified biography and coaching profile;
- understand confirmed specialties, age ranges, session formats, and service area;
- watch an approved social-proof video with accessible captions or transcript;
- see current availability;
- book an available session; or
- request a callback from a human.

The system must support adding, editing, publishing, unpublishing, and marking a coach unavailable without redesigning the site. Bookable coaches and their verified content will be supplied later.

## Boundaries

- The founder/member section and the bookable-coach directory may overlap only when a person is explicitly activated as a private coach.
- Parents or guardians, not minors, complete booking and callback forms.
- No coach is published as available until profile facts, scheduling source, routing destination, consent, and media rights are confirmed.
- A social-proof video must not contain unsupported outcome claims or identifiable minors without appropriate permission.
- “Book” means reserve an appointment; payment timing and cancellation/refund policy remain unresolved.
- The scheduling implementation is not yet selected. A branded SDSA profile with an embedded or connected calendar is preferred over sending families to an uncontextualized third-party profile.
- This decision does not authorize implementation, deployment, or production form submissions.

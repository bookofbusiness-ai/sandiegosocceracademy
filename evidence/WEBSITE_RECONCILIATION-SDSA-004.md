## Reconciled product implication

The rebuild requires two clear pathways within one nonprofit website:

1. **Group training:** explain the current park-district classes and hand families to the verified City of Chula Vista registration destination.
2. **Private training:** help families compare verified coach profiles, select a preferred coach, and submit an inquiry that is routed to that coach or to a documented fallback when the coach is unavailable.

The full supporting website remains in scope: Home, Programs, Private Training, Coaches, About, FAQ, Get Involved, and Contact. Coach records, routing destinations, and donation or support actions require human confirmation before implementation.
